3 simple steps to apply

Effortless, salon-perfect application right at home:

01
Prep & Clean

Push back cuticles with the wooden stick and gently buff nail shine. Clean thoroughly with the alcohol pad.

02
Choose Adhesive

Apply sticky tabs for temporary weekend wear, or brush on a drop of salon glue for 2+ weeks of continuous hold.

03
Press & Hold

Align the press-on nail near your cuticle line and hold down firmly with steady pressure for 20 seconds.
