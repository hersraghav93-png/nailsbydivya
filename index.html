Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my exact nail size for handmade press-ons?

Use a flexible measuring tape or a small piece of tape placed across the widest point of your natural nail bed. Mark the edges with a pen, lay the tape against a ruler, and note the width in millimeters (mm). You can save your measurements directly in your online account.

Do you deliver handmade press-on nails across India?

Yes. Handmade press-on sets are packaged with application glue, adhesive gel tabs, a prep wipe, buffer, and cuticle stick, and shipped across India via express couriers.

Which areas in Delhi NCR do you cover for at-home appointments?

Divya serves selected residential localities across South Delhi, Central Delhi, Gurgaon / Gurugram, Noida, and Aerocity. Exact availability and travel logistics are confirmed during booking consultation.

How long do handmade press-on nails last?

When applied with liquid nail glue, sets last 2 to 3 weeks with proper prep. When applied with adhesive gel tabs, sets last 3 to 7 days and can be removed gently and reused multiple times.

Are at-home appointments automatically booked?

No. All appointments are reviewed and manually confirmed by Divya to ensure travel time, equipment preparation, and design alignment.
