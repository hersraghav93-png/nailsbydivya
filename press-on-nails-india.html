PRESS-ON FAQ

Questions before you order?

Are Nails by Divya press-on nails handmade?

Yes. Each press-on set is handcrafted after your order is confirmed rather than mass-produced as a ready-made stock set.

Do you deliver press-on nails across India?

Yes. Handmade press-on sets are available for delivery across India. Exact delivery timing and charges depend on the destination.

Can I customise a press-on design?

Yes. You can share an inspiration image or ask about changes to an existing collection design. Final design and pricing are confirmed before production.
