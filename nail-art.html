NAIL ART FAQ

Before you choose your design.

Can I bring my own nail-art reference?

Yes. Share your inspiration image or outfit photo on WhatsApp and we will discuss what can be created, along with design-specific pricing before confirmation.

Can nail art be done with extensions?

Yes. Custom nail art can be sculpted onto gel extensions or painted onto natural nails and press-on sets.

Do you offer French nails?

Yes. Classic white tips, coloured French, micro-French, and modern chrome French designs are all part of the service range.

Can you match nails to a wedding lehenga or outfit?

Yes. Outfit matching for weddings, sangeet functions, and festivals is one of Divya's core specialities.
